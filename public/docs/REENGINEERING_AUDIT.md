## 1. AUDIT PURPOSE & ETHICAL BOUNDARIES

This revised audit establishes the verified ground truth of the BHU LOKA repository. It explicitly replaces all previous unverified assumptions with code-level inspection, reproducible test evidence, and strict legal and scientific disclosures.

### Core Re-Engineering Directives:
1. **No Conflation of Heuristics with Neural Model Weights**: The current backend codebase in `Pipelines/bhu_loka_site_analytics_api.py` implements mathematical heuristic band simulation (`synthesize_geospatial_raster`), morphological noise filtering, and polygon vectorization. The full deep neural network (`SegFormer-B0` / `Prithvi-EO`) is a **`PROPOSED`** production pipeline, not a currently deployed runtime model.
2. **Lake-Specific FTL Governance**: Statutory Full Tank Level (FTL) boundaries and buffer protections vary by state water body notification, lake water spread area, and government orders (GOs). Arbitrary universal buffers ($30\text{m}$, $15\text{m}$, $9\text{m}$) are discarded. Where official gazetted FTL boundaries and revenue survey records are unlinked, the platform must display:
   $$\text{STATUS: } \mathbf{LEGAL\ BOUNDARY\ UNVERIFIED}$$
3. **No Automatic Claim of Illegal Encroachment**: Spatial intersections between detected building footprints and water boundaries cannot legally be declared "illegal encroachments" by software alone. All algorithmic overlaps must be labeled:
   $$\mathbf{POTENTIAL\ SPATIAL\ OVERLAP\ —\ REQUIRES\ OFFICIAL\ VERIFICATION}$$
4. **No Arbitrary Buffer Exclusions**: Fixed geometric masks (e.g. $32\text{m}$ railway or $14\text{m}$ road centerlines) are removed unless supported by verified cadastral right-of-way (RoW) asset layers.
5. **Strict Satellite Chronology**:
   - Sentinel-2A was launched on **23 June 2015** (operational late 2015/early 2016); Sentinel-2B was launched on **7 March 2017**. Claims of a "2014 Sentinel-2 image" are scientifically impossible.
   - 2014 historical baselines must strictly utilize **Landsat 8 OLI** (launched 11 February 2013) or contemporaneous high-resolution aerial/satellite archives.
6. **Electronic Evidence & Legal Framework**: Reports generated by BHU LOKA provide preliminary computational spatial intelligence for administrative prioritization. Under the **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA, Section 63)** and the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, automated outputs require physical field verification and certification by competent revenue and survey authorities prior to any judicial or demolition enforcement action.
