NISAR (NASA-ISRO) · Dual-Frequency SAR Perspective

While Sentinel-1 C-band (5.5 cm) successfully penetrates atmospheric clouds, its shorter wavelength scatters primarily off dense crop canopies and tree leaves, causing attenuation over flooded agriculture.

The NISAR Breakthrough (NASA-ISRO SAR):
  • L-band (24 cm, NASA): Longer active microwave wavelength passes directly through tree crowns and dense flooded crop canopies, bouncing off standing floodwater below (double-bounce detection).
  • S-band (9.3 cm, ISRO): Optimized for soil moisture, light vegetation, and active river levee deformation.
  • SweepSAR Architecture: Provides wide 240 km swath with fine 3-10 m spatial resolution and exact 12-day repeat orbit over India.
Source: NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) & ISRO Space Applications Centre (SAC).
