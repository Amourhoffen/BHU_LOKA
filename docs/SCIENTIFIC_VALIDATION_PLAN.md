## 1. SCIENTIFIC & LEGAL FOUNDATIONS

### 1.1 Non-Negotiable Scientific Directives
1. **Explicit Separation of Heuristics from Trained Weights**: Current code in `Pipelines/bhu_loka_site_analytics_api.py` utilizes mathematical synthesis (`synthesize_geospatial_raster`) and deterministic thresholding. Deep neural networks (`SegFormer-B0`, `Prithvi-EO`) are **`PROPOSED ARCHITECTURES`**. All metrics associated with these models are **`PROPOSED BENCHMARKS`**, not achieved operational scores.
2. **Lake-Specific FTL Governance**: Statutory Full Tank Level (FTL) boundaries and buffer protections vary according to individual state irrigation acts, municipal notifications, and lake-specific government orders (GOs). Arbitrary universal buffers ($30\text{m}$, $15\text{m}$, $9\text{m}$) are strictly prohibited. Where official gazetted orders are absent, the platform must display:
   $$\text{STATUS: } \mathbf{LEGAL\ BOUNDARY\ UNVERIFIED}$$
3. **No Automated Determination of Illegal Encroachment**: Algorithmic spatial intersections between remote-sensing building footprints and water boundaries cannot declare an encroachment "illegal". The software flags:
   $$\mathbf{POTENTIAL\ SPATIAL\ OVERLAP\ —\ REQUIRES\ OFFICIAL\ VERIFICATION}$$
4. **No Arbitrary Exclusion Buffers**: Hardcoded exclusion masks (e.g. $32\text{m}$ railway, $14\text{m}$ road centerlines) are removed. Exclusions are only applied when verified cadastral right-of-way (RoW) asset layers are officially loaded.
5. **Strict Satellite Chronology**:
   - 2014 historical decadal baselines must strictly utilize **Landsat 8 OLI** ($30\text{m}$ GSD, launched 11 February 2013).
   - **Sentinel-2 MSI** ($10\text{m}$ GSD) is strictly restricted to its actual operational period (**late 2015 / 2016 to present**). Under no circumstances shall a "2014 Sentinel-2" image be claimed.
6. **Current Indian Electronic Evidence Legal Framework**:
   - The Indian Evidence Act, 1872 has been repealed and replaced by the **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA, Act No. 47 of 2023)**, effective **1 July 2024**.
   - **Section 63 of the BSA** governs the admissibility of electronic records (formerly Section 65B of the IEA).
   - Outputs generated by BHU LOKA are **computational decision-support artifacts**, NOT legally conclusive or court-admissible verdicts. They require physical field inspection, ground survey verification, and certification by competent revenue authorities under BSA Section 63.

## 2. 7-CLASS LULC TAXONOMY & ESA WORLDCOVER HARMONIZATION

### 2.1 Proposed Target LULC Classes
BHU LOKA standardizes on a 7-Class Land Use and Land Cover hierarchy for Indian urban and peri-urban analytics:
- **Class 0 (Background / Unclassified)**: No-data, cloud shadow, or invalid pixels.
- **Class 1 (Built-Up / Buildings)**: Impervious urban fabric, residential/commercial rooftops.
- **Class 2 (Road Networks)**: Paved transportation corridors (highways, arterial roads).
- **Class 3 (Surface Water Bodies)**: Inland lakes, tanks, reservoirs, rivers.
- **Class 4 (Vegetation / Tree Canopy)**: Urban forestry, parks, tree clusters ($>10\%$ crown density).
- **Class 5 (Agricultural Land)**: Cropland, irrigated agricultural parcels, fallow fields.
- **Class 6 (Barren / Open Soil)**: Unvegetated soil, excavation zones, sand deposits, dry beds.

### 2.2 Class Harmonization Mapping with ESA WorldCover (10m)
ESA WorldCover utilizes an 11-class global classification scheme. The classes are **not identical** to the proposed 7-class schema. The following explicit harmonization mapping must be applied during validation:

| ESA WorldCover Label | Code | Mapped BHU LOKA Class | Harmonization Logic & Boundary Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tree cover** | 10 | **Class 4: Vegetation / Tree Canopy** | Direct mapping for woody perennial vegetation. |
| **Shrubland** | 20 | **Class 4: Vegetation** or **Class 6: Barren** | Semiarid scrubland; mapped to Class 4 if $\text{NDVI} \ge 0.25$, otherwise Class 6. |
| **Grassland** | 30 | **Class 4: Vegetation (Low)** | Non-woody pasture and seasonal grass cover. |
| **Cropland** | 40 | **Class 5: Agricultural Land** | Active and seasonal cropland; subject to temporal harvest verification. |
| **Built-up** | 50 | **Class 1: Built-Up / Buildings** | Covers all impervious surfaces. *Note: In WorldCover, roads are absorbed into Built-up 50.* |
| **Bare / sparse vegetation** | 60 | **Class 6: Barren / Open Soil** | Exposed earth, sand, unpaved clearings. |
| **Snow and ice** | 70 | **Class 0: Background / Masked** | Non-applicable to low-altitude tropical Indian urban AOIs; masked. |
| **Permanent water bodies** | 80 | **Class 3: Surface Water Bodies** | Perennial lakes, reservoirs, major river stretches. |
| **Herbaceous wetland** | 90 | **Class 3: Water** / **Class 4: Vegetation** | Water-vegetation ecotone; critical for lake buffer zone assessment. |
| **Mangroves** | 95 | **Class 4: Vegetation (Wetland)** | Coastal woody wetland vegetation. |
| **Moss and lichen** | 100 | **Class 6: Barren / Sparse** | Rare in target Indian metropolitan corridors. |

*Critical Harmonization Caveat*: Road networks (**Class 2**) cannot be resolved solely from $10\text{m}$ WorldCover pixels, because $10\text{m}$ pixels along roads mix asphalt with roadside shoulders and buildings. Therefore, Class 2 validation requires co-registration with high-precision OpenStreetMap vector centerlines or $0.5\text{m}$ VHR orthomosaics.

## 3. PROPOSED AI MODEL ARCHITECTURE & BENCHMARK TARGETS

### 3.1 Proposed Model Backbones (`PROPOSED`)
1. **Lightweight Edge Model**: `SegFormer-B0` (MiT-B0 encoder, lightweight MLP decoder, $3.7\times 10^6$ parameters).
   - Designed for low-latency serverless deployment (CPU/Cloud Run).
2. **Foundation Model Backbone**: `IBM-NASA Prithvi-EO 2.0` (100M parameter Vision Transformer pre-trained on Sentinel-2 HLS multi-spectral time series).
   - Designed for multi-temporal state-level foundation inference.

### 3.2 Evaluation Metrics & Mathematical Formulation
Models will be evaluated using standard spatial cross-validation protocols:

#### Mean Intersection over Union (mIoU)
$$\text{IoU}_c = \frac{TP_c}{TP_c + FP_c + FN_c}, \quad \text{mIoU} = \frac{1}{C}\sum_{c=1}^C \text{IoU}_c$$

#### Overall Accuracy (OA) & Class-Wise F1-Score
$$\text{OA} = \frac{\sum_{c=1}^C TP_c}{\sum_{c=1}^C (TP_c + FP_c + FN_c + TN_c)}$$
$$F_{1, c} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{Precision}_c \cdot \text{Recall}_c}{\text{Precision}_c + \text{Recall}_c} = \frac{2 \cdot TP_c}{2 \cdot TP_c + FP_c + FN_c}$$

### 3.3 Proposed Performance Benchmarks (`PROPOSED BENCHMARK`)

## 4. LAKE FTL & POTENTIAL OVERLAP AUDITING WORKFLOW

### 4.1 Lake-Specific Legal Order Integration
To ensure legal defensibility for Telangana Challenge 03 (HYDRAA):
1. **Official Boundary Storage**: For every lake entity in the database, the system must store:
   - Lake Name & Unique Identifier (e.g. `TS-HYD-LAKE-0042: Hussain Sagar`)
   - Gazetted Water Spread Area (Acres / Hectares)
   - Official Government Order (e.g. `G.O. Ms. No. 168 MA&UD Dept`, `Telangana Irrigation Act`)
   - Survey Authority (e.g. `HMDA / HYDRAA / Irrigation Dept`)
   - Applicable Statutory Buffer Distance ($m$) specified by the applicable notification (e.g. $30\text{m}$ for lakes $>10\text{ha}$, or custom surveyed boundary)
   - Gazette Notification Date & Effective Status
2. **Fallback Protocol**: If the lake record lacks an official gazette notification, the system renders:
   $$\text{STATUS: } \mathbf{LEGAL\ BOUNDARY\ UNVERIFIED}$$
   No legal compliance or violation claims may be rendered.

### 4.2 Potential Spatial Overlap Calculation
Let $\mathcal{P}_{\text{FTL}}$ represent the gazetted Full Tank Level boundary.  
Let $\mathcal{B}_{\text{statutory}}$ represent the buffer geometry specified in the official lake notification.  
The total protected zone is:
$$\mathcal{Z}_{\text{protected}} = \mathcal{P}_{\text{FTL}} \cup \mathcal{B}_{\text{statutory}}$$

For each detected structural footprint $\mathcal{F}_i$:
$$\mathcal{O}_i = \mathcal{F}_i \cap \mathcal{Z}_{\text{protected}}$$
- If $\text{Area}(\mathcal{O}_i) > 0$: The footprint is flagged as:
  $$\mathbf{POTENTIAL\ SPATIAL\ OVERLAP\ —\ REQUIRES\ OFFICIAL\ VERIFICATION}$$
- Overlap Metrics Displayed:
  - Estimated Overlap Area: $\text{Area}(\mathcal{O}_i)\text{ m}^2$ / Acres
  - Centroid Coordinates: $(\text{Lat}, \text{Lon})$ to 6 decimal places ($\approx 0.1\text{m}$ resolution)
  - Estimated Overlap Percentage: $\frac{\text{Area}(\mathcal{O}_i)}{\text{Area}(\mathcal{F}_i)} \times 100\%$
  - Warning Badge: `PENDING FIELD REVENUE SURVEY`

## 5. BI-TEMPORAL DECADAL CHANGE DETECTION (2014 VS 2026)

### 5.1 Verified Sensor Selection
- **$t_1$ Historical Baseline (2014)**:
  - Sensor: **Landsat 8 OLI** (Band 2 Blue, Band 3 Green, Band 4 Red, Band 5 NIR)
  - Level: Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Reflectance (L2SR)
  - Resolution: $30\text{m}$ GSD
  - Cloud Filter: $\le 10\%$ scene cloud cover
- **$t_2$ Current Surveillance Pass (2026)**:
  - Sensor: **Sentinel-2 MSI** (Band 2 Blue, Band 3 Green, Band 4 Red, Band 8 NIR)
  - Level: Level-2A Bottom-Of-Atmosphere (BOA) Reflectance
  - Resolution: $10\text{m}$ GSD
  - Cloud Filter: $\le 15\%$ cloud probability via Scene Classification Layer (SCL)

### 5.2 Resolution Resampling & Co-Registration Protocol
Because Landsat 8 is $30\text{m}$ and Sentinel-2 is $10\text{m}$, direct pixel differencing without interpolation creates aliasing artifacts:
1. **Resampling**: The $30\text{m}$ Landsat 8 bands must be resampled to $10\text{m}$ using bilinear or cubic convolution interpolation aligned to the Sentinel-2 Military Grid Reference System (MGRS) tile grid.
2. **Normalized Multi-Spectral Indices**:
   $$\text{NDWI}_{t1} = \frac{\rho_{G, t1} - \rho_{\text{NIR}, t1}}{\rho_{G, t1} + \rho_{\text{NIR}, t1}}, \quad \text{NDWI}_{t2} = \frac{\rho_{G, t2} - \rho_{\text{NIR}, t2}}{\rho_{G, t2} + \rho_{\text{NIR}, t2}}$$
   $$\Delta\text{NDWI} = \text{NDWI}_{t2} - \text{NDWI}_{t1}$$
3. **Change Classification**:
   - $\Delta\text{NDWI} < -0.25$: Water contraction / Potential shoreline dryout
   - $\Delta\text{NDBI} > +0.20$: New structural emergence / Potential built-up encroachment

## 6. LEGAL EVIDENTIARY FRAMEWORK & COMPLIANCE (BSA 2023)

### 6.1 Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA Section 63)
In accordance with Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023:
1. **Electronic Record Integrity**: Generated dossiers store cryptographic SHA-256 hashes of:
   - Raw input satellite raster files (`GeoTIFF`)
   - Output spatial vector layers (`GeoJSON`)
   - Metadata payload (Satellite mission, UTC timestamp, spatial extent)
2. **Hardware & Environment Attribution**: The dossier records the host system hostname, microservice version, and processing timestamp.
3. **Legal Disclaimer**: Every report generated by BHU LOKA must display the mandatory legal notice:
   > **LEGAL NOTICE**: This document constitutes a preliminary computational Earth observation analysis generated for administrative screening and spatial planning. It does not constitute a judicial finding or legally conclusive proof of encroachment. In accordance with the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (Section 63) and applicable state revenue codes, all flagged spatial overlaps must be verified on the ground by certified government revenue surveyors utilizing official cadastral tippons and gazetted revenue village maps.
